## Experience
---


When I first applied for a CCTV technician position, I thought my days would purely consist of mounting cameras and running cables. I quickly found out it was much more than that! Whenever field work was slow, I was tasked with helping manage network connections and devices across the entire building. Suddenly, I was dealing with dropped printers, misbehaving cameras, and connection glitches. Because my supervisor handled the heavy-duty system administration, I didn't have full admin control—but I still needed a way to assist. That’s where `cmd` saved the day. By opening up the command line, I could instantly check IP addresses, verify if devices were online, and see what was connected to the network. It gave me the visibility I needed to track down issues and give my supervisor accurate information, turning me from an overwhelmed newcomer into a genuinely helpful asset to the team.
